Goal: Build Everflow — an AI-native, self-hosted developer platform combining PaaS deployment (Dokploy/Coolify-style), agentic AI development studio (Lovable/Claude Code-style), n8n-style workflow automation, organizational guardrails, and data sovereignty.

Target users: Development teams and organizations that want to self-host a complete AI-powered DevOps + engineering platform on their own infrastructure.

Core promise:
- Attach any machine with one command
- Build apps by chatting ("vibe code")
- Deploy anywhere with one click
- AI agents that respect org policies
- Live desktop control via Selkies webtop
- Everything runs on your nodes, in your regions
